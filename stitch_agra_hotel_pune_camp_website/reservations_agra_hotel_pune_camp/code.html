Frequently Asked Questions

Planning Your Visit to Agra Hotel

Everything you need to know before joining us for lunch or dinner.

Do we need to pay an advance deposit for table reservations? expand_more

No advance deposit is needed for standard tables up to 8 guests. For large banquet bookings exceeding 12 guests or tailored celebratory menus, a modest token advance may be requested by our coordinator.

Is there a pure vegetarian dining menu available? expand_more

Yes, absolutely. We prepare our vegetarian curries, paneer specialties, dals, and vegetable biryanis using dedicated cookware and separate tandoori skewers with immaculate hygiene standards.

Can we bring our own celebratory cake for birthdays and anniversaries? expand_more

You are warmly welcome to bring your celebration cake. Our stewards will provide celebratory plates, cake-cutting cutlery, and complimentary chilled storage during your meal.

Need urgent booking for tonight? For immediate tables within the next 2 hours, call our floor captain directly.
