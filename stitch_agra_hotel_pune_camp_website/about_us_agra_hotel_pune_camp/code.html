history_edu The Cantonment Heritage

Rooted Where Soldiers, Scholars & Families Broke Bread

In the bustling cantonment quarter of Pune during the mid-20th century, Agra Hotel opened its timber doors with a singular commitment: honoring ancestral North Indian and royal Mughlai culinary techniques. Pune Camp was alive with the cadence of military brass bands, vintage bicycle bells, and the aromatic smoke of charcoal tandoors drifting across East Street and Camp Road.

Through changing decades and evolving skylines, Agra Hotel has preserved the sanctity of unhurried dining. Here, traditional biryani pots simmer patiently on charcoal embers, naans are hand-slapped against scorching clay walls, and every broth is reduced gently until pure fragrance envelops the wooden dining room.
